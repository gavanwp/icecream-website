It started with a broken freezer.

In 2019, our founder's freezer died in a heatwave, and a bucket of strawberries turned into the best soft-serve she'd ever tasted. She spent a year turning that accident into a recipe.

Today we churn small batches every morning in our shop. When a flavor sells out, it's gone until tomorrow.
